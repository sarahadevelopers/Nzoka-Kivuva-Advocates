Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about contacting and working with our firm

How do I schedule a consultation?

You can schedule a consultation by calling our office during business hours or by filling out the contact form. We respond within 24 hours on business days.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Please bring any relevant documents related to your legal matter, including contracts, court papers, and identification documents.

Do you handle cases outside of Kitengela?

Yes, we handle cases throughout Kenya. While our physical office is in Kitengela, we regularly represent clients from other counties.
