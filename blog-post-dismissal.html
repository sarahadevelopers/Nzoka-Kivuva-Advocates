Unfair Dismissal: When Can You Challenge Your Termination?

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Article
Featured Image
April 20, 2024 By Advocate Nzoka 5 min read

Employment termination can be devastating. Learn about what constitutes unfair dismissal under Kenyan law, the remedies available, and the process for filing a claim with the Employment and Labour Relations Court.

Substantive and Procedural Fairness

Under Section 45 of the Employment Act 2007, an employer must prove that the termination was for a valid reason and that the employee was given a fair hearing.

What is Unfair?

  • Dismissal without a valid reason (e.g., discrimination, retaliation).
  • Failure to follow a proper disciplinary process.
  • Dismissal for union membership or pregnancy.

Your Remedies

If you succeed, the Employment and Labour Relations Court can order Reinstatement, Re-engagement, or Compensation of up to 12 months' salary.
