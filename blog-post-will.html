Writing a Valid Will in Kenya: Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Article
Featured Image
January 15, 2023 By Advocate Nzoka 5 min read

A properly drafted will ensures your assets are distributed according to your wishes. This guide explains the legal requirements for a valid will in Kenya and common pitfalls that can lead to disputes among beneficiaries.

Legal Requirements

Under the Law of Succession Act, a will must be in writing, signed by the testator in the presence of two or more witnesses, and the witnesses must sign in the presence of the testator.

Common Mistakes

  • Verbal Wills: Intestate succession applies if the will is not written, leading to court battles.
  • No Mental Capacity: If a will is drafted when the testator is mentally incapacitated, it is void.
  • Not Updating: Failing to update your will after marriage, divorce, or new children.

Protecting Your Loved Ones

We guide you through the entire process, ensuring your will is legally airtight, reducing the risk of future litigation among beneficiaries.
