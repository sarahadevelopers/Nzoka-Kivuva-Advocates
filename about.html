Who We Are

A firm built on integrity and accessible justice.

Nzoka Kivuva & Associates Advocates is a law firm based in Kitengela, Kajiado County, providing legal representation, advisory and transactional services to individuals, families, businesses and institutions.

Our practice is built around a simple principle: every client deserves clear legal guidance, diligent representation and professional service.

We approach each matter with careful attention to its circumstances, helping our clients understand their legal position, assess their options and move forward with confidence.

The Founder

Adv. Nzoka Kivuva

Founder & Managing Partner

An Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, Nzoka Kivuva was admitted to the bar in 2019 after graduating from the Catholic University of Eastern Africa.

He leads the firm with a commitment to accessible, high-quality legal representation and a client-focused approach to legal practice.
