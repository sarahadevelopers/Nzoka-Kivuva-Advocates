Essential Contracts Every Small Business Needs in Kenya

  • Home

October 5, 2024 By Advocate Nzoka 6 min read

Protecting your business starts with proper legal documentation. From employment agreements to service contracts, well-drafted contracts are your first line of defense against disputes.

The Core Contracts You Need

1. Employment Agreements

Written employment contracts outline the terms of employment, salaries, leave, and termination clauses. They are mandatory under the Employment Act 2007.

2. Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

If your business deals with sensitive information or proprietary processes, NDAs protect your trade secrets from leaking to competitors.

3. Service Agreements

These are crucial for protecting your business when outsourcing work. They should clearly define deliverables, timelines, fees, and Dispute Resolution mechanisms.

"A verbal contract isn't worth the paper it's written on."

Review is Key

We advise business owners to have these contracts reviewed annually to ensure they remain compliant with changing Kenyan laws and reflect the current scale of their operations.
