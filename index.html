Nzoka Kivuva & Associates Advocates

Nzoka Kivuva & Associates Advocates is a Kitengela-based law firm dedicated to providing strategic, client-focused legal services. With expertise across personal, family, property and business law, we tailor our legal counsel to the real needs of every individual or organization we serve.

Our firm is built on the foundation that justice should be accessible to all, regardless of status or circumstance. We combine legal excellence with compassionate understanding of our clients' situations.

With a commitment to integrity, clarity, and justice, we guide our clients through complex legal challenges with honesty and skill, ensuring their rights are protected and their voices heard.

Initial Consultation FAQs

Answers to common questions before you reach out to our team.

How do I book a consultation? +
You can book directly through our contact page, or simply call or WhatsApp us at +254 710 599 653. We will schedule a convenient time for you.
How much do your services typically cost? +
Consultation fees are discussed upfront. For specific matters, we provide clear estimates based on the complexity and scope of the work required, ensuring no hidden surprises.
How long does a property transfer take? +
It depends on the state of the title, the consent process, and government processing times. We guide you step-by-step to keep timelines as short as possible.
Will I be kept updated on my case? +
Absolutely. We believe in complete transparency. You will receive structured updates on the progress of your matter at every stage.
