Land Title Transfers in Kenya: A Step-by-Step Guide

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Article
Featured Image
January 15, 2025 By Advocate Nzoka 6 min read

Navigating the process of transferring land titles in Kenya can be complex, but with the right legal guidance, it becomes a straightforward journey. This comprehensive guide outlines the legal requirements, documentation needed, and common pitfalls to avoid when transferring property ownership.

The Importance of Due Diligence

Before initiating any transfer, it is crucial to conduct a thorough search at the Ministry of Lands. This ensures the title is free from encumbrances, caveats, or illegal allocations. Our office ensures that all searches are verified to protect our clients from fraud.

Step 1: Preparation of Documents

You will need to prepare the original title deed, national IDs, KRA PIN certificates, and the signed sale agreement. Additionally, if the property is owned by a company, we will need the certificate of incorporation and relevant resolutions.

"A properly executed transfer is the foundation of secure property ownership. Never attempt to transfer land without proper legal vetting."

Step 2: Lodging the Transfer

  • Execution of the transfer instrument by both parties.
  • Payment of stamp duty at the prescribed rate.
  • Submission of the application to the land registry.
  • Consent from the Land Control Board (if applicable).

Step 3: Registration and Issuance

Once the Registrar processes the transfer, a new title deed is issued in the name of the buyer. This process can take several weeks, depending on the efficiency of the specific registry.
